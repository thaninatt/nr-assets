User Data Deletion Instructions

Day Zero Poster application, operated by Next Rocket Agency. Last updated 1 September 2026.

What data exists

Day Zero Poster is an internal publishing tool. It holds access tokens for social media accounts that Next Rocket Agency owns, the content the agency prepares for publication, and a log of publication results. It does not hold personal data belonging to members of the public.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to thanin@nextrocket.co.th with the subject line "Data deletion request - Day Zero Poster".
  2. State the account or the specific content the request covers, so the correct records can be located.
  3. Next Rocket Agency confirms receipt within 7 days and completes the deletion within 30 days of confirmation.

Removing the application's access

An administrator of a connected Facebook Page or Instagram account can remove this application's access at any time through Facebook settings, under Business Integrations. Removing access stops all further publishing by the application immediately.
