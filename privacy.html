Privacy Policy

Day Zero Poster application, operated by Next Rocket Agency. Last updated 1 September 2026.

1. What this application is

Day Zero Poster is an internal publishing tool built and used by Next Rocket Agency. It publishes content that Next Rocket Agency owns to social media accounts that Next Rocket Agency owns and administers, including its Facebook Page, its Instagram professional account, and its Threads profile.

The application is not offered to the public. There is no sign up, no login for outside users, and no consumer facing feature.

2. Data we collect

The application does not collect personal data from members of the public. The only data it handles is:

  • Access tokens for the Facebook Page, Instagram account, and Threads profile that Next Rocket Agency owns. Tokens are stored on a company controlled machine and are never shared with third parties.
  • Content that Next Rocket Agency creates for publication, such as video files, image files, and captions.
  • Publication results returned by Meta, such as post identifiers and timestamps, kept as an internal log.

The application does not read private messages, does not build profiles of individuals, and does not sell or transfer any data.

3. How the data is used

Data is used for one purpose only, which is to publish and keep records of Next Rocket Agency's own social media content.

4. Data retention

Publication logs are kept for internal reference and are deleted when no longer needed. Access tokens are revoked when the integration is retired.

5. Third parties

The application communicates with Meta Platforms services, namely the Facebook Graph API, the Instagram Graph API, and the Threads API, to publish content. No other third party receives data from this application.

7. Data deletion

Instructions for requesting deletion of data held by this application are published at data-deletion.html.
